ORBO V8 - INTERNAL STRUCTURE FOR PRINTING AND ASSEMBLY

14 separate STL files. Print one of each needed part.
Units: millimetres. Use 100% scale.
Import as separate parts, not as one fused object. Choose print orientation
and supports in your slicer; these exports retain their CAD orientations.

FRAME HARDWARE
- Four M3 x 80 socket screws, four M3 flat washers (modeled 0.5 mm thick)
  and four M3 hex nuts (modeled 2.4 mm thick) for the main frame stack.
- Four M3 x 12 socket screws for the servo feet.
- Four M3 x 10 socket screws for the neck-carrier-to-servo-tower ties.
- Two M3 x 10 socket screws for the shelf-to-front-frame ties.
- Ten M3 inserts: four deck, four tower seats, two front-frame seats.
  The modeled pilots are diameter 4.2 mm with nominal 5 mm insert length.
  Match the pilots to the actual inserts you use before printing.
- Servo ears retain the V7 arrangement: four M2 x 5 screws and short inserts.
- Arm hubs retain four M2 x 10 screws for the provisional horn pilot holes.
- Retain the existing motor/wheel, caster and mast hardware from the project.
- The eight old M3 x 30 frame spacers are replaced by this braced frame.

ASSEMBLY ORDER
1. Prepare the inserts and clear the through-bolt holes in the printed parts.
2. Attach motors, wheels and casters to the motor plate. Insert the four
   M3 x 80 frame bolts upward from underneath. Support their heads on the bench.
3. Lower the front and rear frames over the bolts onto the plate support pads.
   The front frame's diagonal supports point inward beneath the battery shelf.
4. Fit the battery shelf. Tighten its two M3 x 10 screws into the front frame.
   Fit the battery and its retention strap.
5. Attach the U2D2 and regulator beneath the electronics cage on the bench;
   connect their cables. Lower the cage over the long bolts onto the shelf.
   Fit the four top washers/nuts and tighten evenly without distorting prints.
6. If fitting the outer shell now, raise it from underneath and fasten its
   underside ledges BEFORE fitting the servo modules, Pi and neck carrier.
   The shell is deliberately excluded from this print package.
7. Fit each servo/horn to its holder. Lower each module with its holder
   6 mm inward from its final position, seat its foot, then slide it outward
   6 mm and fasten it with two M3 x 12 screws. Leave the Pi and carrier off
   during this step to keep the screw access clear.
8. Install the Pi/cooler and connect wiring. Keep cables clear of moving
   parts, cooling airflow and the concealed screwdriver passages.
9. Fit the mast/bushing and internal neck carrier. Start all four M3 x 10
   tower-tie screws and the central support connection before tightening.
10. Fit the keyed arm hubs to the horns. Install the exterior covers/head
    later using the existing project instructions.

For later shell installation/removal, first remove the neck carrier, Pi,
arm hubs and servo modules to restore the checked shell-insertion configuration.
The completed core can stay assembled while the empty lower shell is fitted.

FIT CHECK
The accepted servo envelope is provisional: 23 x 12.2 x 24 mm.
All included meshes are watertight. CAD assembly/tool-access checks passed.
Check actual servo tabs/horn, inserts, strap/cable clearances and print fit.
Printed stiffness and insert retention still require a prototype check.
