Try a mood

Calm. A steady aqua glow, and slow blinks.

  • A face that changes

    Two bright eyes on dark glass. They blink, squint and widen.

  • Flippers that wave

    Each one moves on its own. Hello, goodbye, look at this.

  • Wheels, tucked away

    Two hidden wheels let Orbo roll and turn on the spot.

  • A puff on a stalk

    The stalk pulls out. The puff swaps for another.

  • A ring of light

    One thin band around the neck. Its colour is the mood.

  • A belly that sees

    A small camera to look around, and a warm lamp below it.

Is the $199 really refundable?

Yes. Ask for it back at any time before your Orbo ships and you get the full $199. No fee, no form to argue with.

What will Orbo cost in full?

The final price is not set yet. We will tell you before we ask for anything more. If the number is not right for you, your deposit goes back.

When does it ship?

There is no date yet. Orbo is still in development and we would rather be honest than early. People with a reservation hear first and ship first.

How do the two editions differ?

Orbo arrives assembled and ready, and you reserve it with a refundable $199 deposit. Orbo Open is free. Its design files and code are on GitHub, for people who want to build, repair and reprogram their own. The robot is the same.

What does the waitlist do?

It is free. You get progress updates, the film when it is done, and a note when orders open. You can leave at any time.

How far along is Orbo today?

The design is final and a prototype sits on our bench. Its behaviour is still being built. We will show each step as it comes together.
